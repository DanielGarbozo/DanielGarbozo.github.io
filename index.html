About

Reproducible software for reading the genome, in one dimension and in three.

I'm a Peruvian bioinformatician building production-grade, reproducible software for large-scale omics: DSL2 Nextflow pipelines with Docker and Singularity profiles, and R and Python packages.

My work covers the path from raw sequencing reads to multi-omics integration (RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, ATAC-seq, Hi-C and single-nucleus RNA-seq), with an eye on the biology behind the data and growing experience integrating AI tools. Over three-plus years I have done bioinformatics research with the University of Pennsylvania, New York University, The Rockefeller University, Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health and BigMind, and I have led the bioinformatics component of studies across laboratories and institutions. I care about collaborative teams, including mentoring peers.

Next, I want to expand into machine learning and deep learning that integrate genomics with clinical data and images as additional layers.

Research

The questions I work on.

One card per question. Projects with unpublished manuscripts are described by question and method only.

Ercan Lab · NYU Center for Genomics and Systems Biology · 2025-now

How do cohesin and condensin fold chromosomes during dosage compensation?

Manuscript in preparation

In C. elegans, I build HPC workflows on Hi-C and ChIP-seq data to study chromatin fountains, loop extrusion, X-versus-autosome interaction profiles, 60° cone profiles and site-centered strength at rex regions, with differential binding across cohesin, condensin and RNA Pol II perturbations and RNA-seq after SMC-3/WAPL depletion.

  • Hi-C
  • ChIP-seq
  • cooltools
  • coolpuppy
  • DiffBind
  • DESeq2

dryhic · own project

Can a model trained on simulations read fountains from a single Hi-C map?

In development

Fountain strength is continuous, and each published caller cuts it at a different point. I am building a transformer that scores fountain anchors as calibrated per-bin probabilities from one contact map, pretrained on parametric fountain simulators instead of published calls, then benchmarked on published anchors in a CTCF-free genome without fine-tuning.

  • Transformers
  • Simulation-based learning
  • cooler
  • Python

Risca Lab · The Rockefeller University · 2025

How does chromatin accessibility change when a histone variant is lost under stress?

I ran DiffBind differential-accessibility analysis of ATAC-seq data from macroH2A knockdown versus wild-type RPE cells, with and without TGF-β1 stress, followed by TF motif analysis (JASPAR, HOCOMOCO), ChIPseeker annotation and deepTools signal and metagene profiles.

Related and ongoing: genomic analysis of DNA double-strand break sites in yeast, with the Bernstein Lab at the University of Pennsylvania.

  • ATAC-seq
  • DiffBind
  • Motif analysis
  • deepTools

Gilman Lab · Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health · 2024

What does the blood transcriptome reveal in early chronic Chagas cardiomyopathy?

Manuscript submitted

I led the RNA-seq analysis of a blood-transcriptome study: read QC and trimming (FastQC, MultiQC, BBDuk), quantification with Salmon and tximport, differential expression with DESeq2, unsupervised structure (PCA, UMAP, t-SNE, HDBSCAN) and pathway enrichment across MSigDB, plus manuscript figures and Methods/Results writing.

  • RNA-seq
  • Salmon
  • DESeq2
  • MSigDB

What can platelets tell us about tuberculosis?

Manuscript in preparation

With Daniela E. Kirwan, Ph.D., I lead a platelet transcriptomics study of tuberculosis: RNA-seq from raw reads through Salmon and DESeq2, followed by competitive pathway enrichment with CAMERA, PADOG and GSEA.

Independent reanalysis of public data · GEO GSE272719

Do sex differences in brain cell types hold up across species and methods?

Public repository

A reproducible reanalysis of a Peromyscus preoptic-area snRNA-seq atlas. I rebuilt 53 neuron clusters from 95,057 nuclei of two species and mapped them onto the mouse POA MERFISH atlas (held-out accuracy 0.73 inhibitory, 0.81 excitatory). Treating the published sex-bias result as a hypothesis, the between-species ratio of male-biased genes held between 1.4 and 2.7 across subsampling schemes, gene filters and two methods (edgeR, PyDESeq2), against a published 2.8.

  • scanpy
  • Harmony
  • Scrublet
  • edgeR
  • PyDESeq2

FARVET Bioinformatics Core · 2026-now

How do surface proteins of poultry viruses vary among Peruvian field isolates?

Manuscript in preparation

For Newcastle disease virus, I study the pre-fusion F glycoprotein of Peruvian field isolates with molecular dynamics. For avian orthoreovirus, I built a surveillance workflow that genotypes isolates by the σC attachment gene from Oxford Nanopore consensus sequences: codon-aware alignment (MACSE), maximum-likelihood trees (IQ-TREE, cross-checked in MEGA), AlphaFold2-Multimer trimer models benchmarked against an RMSD noise floor from crystal structures (bio3d), and surface-exposure (SASA) mapping.

  • Molecular dynamics
  • AlphaFold2-Multimer
  • IQ-TREE
  • Nanopore

BigMind · OmicsKit · research interest

How can omics be integrated with clinical data and images?

My long-term interest is integrative foundation models that combine genomics with clinical data and imaging. The groundwork is reusable infrastructure: OmicsKit, an R package for multi-omics analysis and visualization that brings annotation, dimensionality reduction, pathway enrichment, gene-set networks, ROC/AUC, Kaplan-Meier survival and Cox forest plots into one toolkit.

Software

Tools I've built and contributed to.

Tested, documented and versioned. One card per repository.

hicpup

Author · Python · cooltools, coolpuppy, pytest, GitHub Actions

Downstream analysis of Hi-C pileups: fountain cone profiles, P(s) slopes and loop quantification. A modular package with a four-command Typer CLI (expected, pileup, profile, plot), explicit I/O and zero global state.

Regression tests (frozen-array, synthetic signal-injection, NaN-contamination); CI runs ruff and pytest on every push; type-hinted docs on GitHub Pages.

GitHub Docs

hicpup-nf

Author · Nextflow DSL2 · Docker, Singularity, GitHub Actions

A modular DSL2 pipeline (HIC2COOL → EXPECTED → PILEUP → PROFILE → PLOT), one container per process, following the nf-core pattern. Combinable profiles separate engine from dataset (test/docker, full/singularity).

CI with a no-NaN content assertion; handles .hic and .cool inputs, scaling from synthetic fixtures to public GEO data.

GitHub

peroPOA_snRNAseq

Author (solo project) · Python, R · scanpy, Harmony, Scrublet, PyDESeq2, Seurat, edgeR

A reproducible single-nucleus RNA-seq reanalysis pipeline, from GEO count matrices through QC, integration, clustering, MERFISH label transfer, pseudobulk differential abundance and expression, and a robustness check across subsampling and methods.

GitHub

OmicsKit

Core developer · R · Ensembl BioMart · BigMind

Manuscript submitted

An R package for reproducible multi-omics analysis and visualization. Reusable functions for annotation, dimensionality reduction, differential-analysis visualization, pathway enrichment (GSEA, CAMERA, PADOG), gene-set networks, ROC/AUC and Kaplan-Meier plots, with version-aware, multi-species BioMart annotation.

GitHub

ercantools

Contributor · R · Ercan Lab

Reusable plotting and analysis utilities for condensin and dosage-compensation ChIP-seq workflows in C. elegans, built on DiffBind output.

GitHub

SeqWrangler (ClawBio)

Open-source contributor · Python · advisor Manuel Corpas, Ph.D.

A Python pipeline for automated NGS read processing with multi-aligner support (BWA, Bowtie2, minimap2), a synthetic-data demo mode, and mock-based tests that emit reproducibility artifacts (metadata, SHA-256 checksums).

GitHub

Teloscope documentation

Documentation contributor · MkDocs, Read the Docs · Vertebrate Genome Lab

Designed and deployed a 10-section MkDocs and Read the Docs site for a published telomere-annotation tool, reusing the repository README as a single source of truth, with a Material-theme configuration managed through a fork/upstream release workflow.
